About

I'm happiest when a curious question turns into something I can actually build, wire up, and run.

It usually starts with a simple “why does this work?” From there I follow the thread, from the circuit on the board to the code telling it what to do, until the two finally make sense together. That meeting point between hardware and software is where I like to spend my time.

I’m still early in the journey, and honestly I like it that way. I’d rather try something unfamiliar and learn from the mistakes than wait until I feel ready. New tools, new ideas, and new people to build with all make me a little sharper.

Away from the breadboard, my creative side takes over, and it has a habit of finding its way back into my engineering.
